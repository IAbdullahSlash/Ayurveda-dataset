# Permission and data-access request letters

Ready-to-send templates for the five organisations whose data would most strengthen AyurKB but which cannot be
redistributed (or only partly) under their current terms. Fill the `[bracketed]` fields, print on institutional
letterhead if you have one, and send by e-mail or post. Keep copies of the replies in `docs/permission_requests/replies/`
and record the outcome in `data/processed/sources.csv` (licence column) before adding any new data.

| # | Organisation | What we ask for | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Pharmacopoeia Commission for Indian Medicine & Homoeopathy (PCIM&H) | machine-readable API monographs + AFI formulations, permission for a derived non-commercial dataset | authoritative herb quality standards and ~1,000 official formulations (AyurKB has 43 curated) |
| 2 | Ministry of Ayush - NAMASTE portal team | explicit licence for NAMASTE codes/SAT; corrected Devanagari/diacritic columns | NAMASTE is the backbone of AyurKB; current files have no stated licence and some encoding defects |
| 3 | WHO (Traditional, Complementary & Integrative Medicine unit / IRIS permissions) | confirmation that the adapted terminology tables may be redistributed under CC BY-NC-SA; machine-readable source | removes PDF-parsing error risk (reported issues attached) |
| 4 | IMPPAT team, The Institute of Mathematical Sciences (IMSc), Chennai | permission to include plant-phytochemical associations in derived form (CC BY-NC-ND forbids derivatives) | 17,967 phytochemicals of Indian medicinal plants with structures |
| 5 | Central Council for Research in Ayurvedic Sciences (CCRAS) | de-identified clinical study data / AYUSH Research Portal metadata | real-world diagnosis-treatment-outcome data for validating the expert system |

Do not send these letters on behalf of an institution without its approval. Do not include patient data in any request.
