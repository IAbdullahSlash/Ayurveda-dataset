AyurKB आयुर्वेद ज्ञानकोश

A bilingual (English · हिन्दी, with Sanskrit in IAST and Devanagari), hybrid Ayurveda knowledge base for expert systems. It joins official terminology, materia medica, remedies and modern evidence, then benchmarks machine-learning models on it. Only authoritative or openly licensed sources are used.

One concept, four scripts

What a record looks like

Every disorder, symptom, herb and term carries its Sanskrit form (IAST and Devanagari), its Hindi usage form and English, plus the codes needed to link it to other systems.

Evidence behind each link

Literature and classical support

Every herb → disorder and formulation → disorder link was searched in PubMed (plant or formulation name with a curated biomedical query for the disorder). Tiers: clinical study (trial, RCT, meta-analysis or systematic review), literature (3+ papers, co-occurring at least twice as often as chance), sparse, none. Co-occurrence is a pointer to evidence, not proof of efficacy.
