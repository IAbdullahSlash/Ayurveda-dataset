Prakṛti questionnaire

24 questions about traits you have had most of your life. The answers are scored the classical way, by counting vāta, pitta and kapha features, using items grounded in the WHO Ayurveda terminology (ITA-2.8.3 to 2.8.5).

Before you start

  • This is an educational tool, not a diagnosis. For any health concern, see a qualified Ayurveda practitioner or doctor.
  • Answer for how you usually are, not how you feel today. If unsure, choose "Not sure".
  • Your answers stay on this page. They are stored only if the study team saves them, with a random response code and no name, e-mail or account details.
  • You can ask the study team to delete a saved response by quoting its code.
Please confirm you are 18 or older to begin.

Your result

A questionnaire estimate only. Prakṛti assessment by a practitioner also uses examination (darśana, sparśana, praśna) and history.
